About

Threat Intel combines two public, keyless vulnerability sources, joined on CVE ID.

CISA KEV

Which vulnerabilities are being exploited in the wild. Loaded in bulk when a logged-in user refreshes, at most once an hour for the whole app.

NIST NVD

CVSS scores and analysis status. Fetched the first time a CVE's page is viewed, then cached for 30 days once analyzed, or 24 hours otherwise.

CVSS scores from different versions (v4.0, v3.1, v3.0, v2) aren't directly comparable, so each score is shown with its version.

Logged-in users can keep a private watchlist with a note for each CVE.
