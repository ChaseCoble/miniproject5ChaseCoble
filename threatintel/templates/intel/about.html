About

Threat Intel aggregates two public, keyless sources, joined on CVE ID:

  • CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV): which vulnerabilities are being exploited in the wild. Loaded in bulk when a logged-in user refreshes, at most once an hour.
  • NIST National Vulnerability Database (NVD): CVSS scores, fetched the first time a CVE's page is viewed and cached.

Logged-in users can keep a private watchlist with a note for each CVE.
